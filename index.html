Tower of Hanoi

Solve a three-ring puzzle on your own, then a five-ring puzzle with Reachy. Both rounds use three pegs. You have five minutes for each round.

Select two pegs or drag a ring to another peg.

Puzzle rules

Goal: Move the entire tower from Peg 1 to Peg 3.

  1. Move one ring at a time, taking only the top ring from a peg.
  2. Never place a larger ring on a smaller one.

With three pegs, each extra ring doubles the minimum number of moves and adds one. Three rings take 7 moves, four take 15, and five take 31.
